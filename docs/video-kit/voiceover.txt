During a memory-care handoff, a note may mention a walker, an aide, and several activities. CareSignal helps reviewers see what the documentation says about one selected walk and check the supporting evidence.

This walkthrough uses fictional residents and fixed fixture results for a repeatable demonstration.

For Elena’s breakfast walk, the note describes an aide beside her. Her staff-confirmed usual level is independent. CareSignal shows more help than usual.

Switch to Ruth. The note stays the same. Her usual level already includes supervision, so her card matches the usual routine.

Open the evidence. The relevant words are highlighted beside the source date, version, and confirmed baseline. Both sources remain available so reviewers can check the finding. Code calculates the comparison.

Now attach a replacement. It mentions a walker but leaves human help unspecified. The result becomes unclear, the old flag clears, and version one stays in history with its original text.

CareSignal also supports Gemini extraction and reproducible evaluation on public synthetic notes. Results and limitations are recorded separately.

This project is a prototype and is not medical advice.
